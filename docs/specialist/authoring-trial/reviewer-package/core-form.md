# Short review form (13 questions)

For each question, write an answer and, where something is wrong, its severity:
- **blocking:** fix before the trial
- **major:** it would distort results
- **minor:** wording or a nice-to-have

Concrete suggestions help most.

## After the practice
1. **Differences.** Where did your practice answers differ from guidelines §16? For each difference, who was
   right, and was the guideline unclear?
2. **Stated, implied or inferred** (§6). Could you tell these apart reliably? Where not?
3. **Recorded or actual** (§12.1). Was the difference between "what the records show" and "what was actually
   the case" clear enough to apply?
4. **Evidence standing** (§8.1).
   - Could you tell the five standings apart?
   - Is "document type alone never establishes authority" workable?
   - Standing is judged for the **originating source**, and the record's limits are tracked separately
     (§8.2). Is that workable, for example for Hana's own statement as transcribed in the minutes?
5. **Weighing** (§12.4). Would two careful people reach the same actual-world answers using the weighing
   procedure, for example for Passage D? What would make it more consistent, without becoming a blanket
   rule?

## Fairness and coverage
6. **Neutrality.** Does anything in the guidelines presuppose a particular kind of software, or favour
   facts that fit neatly in a table or graph over facts that live in prose?
7. **Missing situations.** Which common situations in project documents do the guidelines not cover?
   Examples:
   - acting or deputy roles
   - retroactive changes
   - "failed to deliver"
   - sarcasm
   - deliberate misinformation

## Template and scoring
8. **Template.** Is the spreadsheet template usable? What slowed you down?
9. **Answer contract** (scoring-protocol §2, Appendix A). Could any kind of system, including a person or a
   program that only reads text, fill it in fairly? Are the five uncertainty labels clear?
10. **Credit and evidence** (scoring-protocol §4.2, §4.3).
    - Are the credit values sensible?
    - Should full credit require all the essential evidence, even when the conclusion is right?
11. **Aliases** (scoring-protocol §4.1.1). Are the mechanical matching rules enough? Is any normalisation
    risky, or is anything missing?
12. **System mappings** (system-mappings-v0.md). Does any mapping add reasoning or caution that the system
    did not produce, or hide a loss? Please look closely at the GUPI mapping (R3): its author also wrote
    these guidelines.

## Overall
13. **Ready?** Is the package ready for the trial: yes, with changes, or no? List the changes you consider
    blocking.
