# Practice passages (do these first)

These are the guidelines' own worked examples, **with the answers removed**. Answer them before you read
§16 of the guidelines, where the answers are.

For each passage, write down, in plain words or in the spreadsheet template:
- the statements, with who asserts each and how certain they are
- each statement's evidence standing (§8.1)
- any negation, termination or reinstatement
- for each question:
  - your answer
  - its status (`stated`, `inferred`, `disputed`, `insufficient`, `not-yet-available`)
  - the evidence it needs
  - for actual-world questions, your justification

Then read guidelines §16, and note every difference and who you think is right.
All names and organisations are fictional.

## Passage A
> Email from Wen Abara to Mirela Tanaka, 9 May 2026: "FYI, see below."
> ---- Forwarded message ---- from Ravi Lindqvist, 2 May 2026: "Joaquín told me he's leaving the project at
> the end of the month."

Questions:
1. As of 5 May 2026, could the Authority (Mirela Tanaka) have known that Joaquín planned to leave?

## Passage B
> Minutes, 3 March 2026: "Hana Osei confirmed she is not the safety adviser for the quay works; that remains
> with the contractor."
> Minutes, 17 March 2026: "Joaquín Ferro stepped down as site lead on 10 March for family reasons."
> Email from Wen Abara, Monday 20 April 2026: "Good news: Joaquín is back as site lead from next Monday."

Questions:
1. According to the project records, was Joaquín site lead on 1 April 2026?
2. Was Joaquín actually site lead on 1 April 2026?
3. Who was the safety adviser for the quay works on 3 March 2026?

## Passage C
> Minutes, 10 February 2026: "Tallis started on site on 2 February."
> Email from Ravi Lindqvist, 12 February 2026: "Correction to the minutes: we started on 9 February, not
> 2 February."
> Status table "as of 30 June 2026", row Soil survey: "complete".
> Chat message from Joaquín Ferro, 30 June 2026: "survey still has two boreholes outstanding."

Questions:
1. When did Tallis start on site?
2. Was the soil survey complete on 30 June 2026?

## Passage D
> Email from Tallis Ground Survey Ltd's office, 1 July 2026: "Ravi Lindqvist left Tallis on 30 June. Please
> send survey queries to the office address."
>
> The documents end in July 2026. No later document mentions Ravi.

Questions:
1. Was Ravi actually working for Tallis on 15 July 2026?
2. And on 1 October 2026?

## Passage E
> Email from Tallis's office, 2 October 2026: "Attached is our current register of all survey staff, as at
> 1 October." The register lists six names, not including Ravi Lindqvist.
> Attendance list in the minutes of 5 October 2026: eight names, not including Ravi Lindqvist.
> Chat message from Wen Abara, 6 October 2026: "someone said Ravi's back at Tallis?"

Questions:
1. Was Ravi working for Tallis as survey staff on 1 October 2026?
2. Which of the three items support your answer, and which do not?
